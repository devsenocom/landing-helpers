Privacy Policy

Last updated — 8 July 2025

1. Introduction

Ever Lusting Games (“we,” “us,” “our”) operates a network of promotional landing pages under the domain lustpush.com (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains—in plain terms—what limited information is processed when you visit the Site, how it is used, and the safeguards in place. The Policy forms part of our Terms of Service.

2. Key Principle: We Do Not Collect Personal Data

The Site does not request, store or otherwise process any data that directly identifies you—such as names, e-mails, phone numbers or payment details.

This site promotes fictional fantasy games. No downloads or software installation are initiated or required.

3. Minimal Technical Logs

Web servers generate short-term technical logs containing truncated IP address, user-agent string, referring URL, timestamp and landing-page ID. Logs are kept 30 days for security and aggregate statistics, then irreversibly anonymised or deleted.

4. Cookies & Similar Technologies

The Site sets one first-party cookie: a random non-personal identifier used solely for frequency-capping creatives. It expires after 24 hours and can be blocked or deleted without impacting core functionality.

5. How We Use the Information

  • Security / Anti-Fraud
  • Aggregate Performance Metrics
  • Legal Compliance

6. No Sale or Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade any information. Data is processed only by trusted providers (hosting, CDN, anti-bot) bound by strict confidentiality obligations.

7. Security Measures

The Site is served exclusively over HTTPS; access to raw logs is restricted to authorised personnel under least-privilege principles.

8. International Transfers

Because our CDN operates globally, technical logs may be processed on servers outside your country. Equivalent protections are applied through data-processing agreements and standard contractual clauses.

9. Your Rights

If applicable under GDPR or similar laws, you may request confirmation whether we hold any personal data about you (we should not) and may request deletion of any residual technical log linked to your truncated IP. Send requests to support@lustpush.com; we respond within 30 days.

10. Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition or restructuring, anonymised or aggregate log data may transfer to the successor entity under the same privacy commitments.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy periodically. A new “Last updated” date will signal changes. Continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
